license-verified before import; see `assets/SOURCES.md` for sha256s and the full table.
Credit below is courtesy — the Unlicense requires none.

but nothing has been taken from them yet.

## Animal Garden — Lion (aquarius_playz)

The lion's 34-part model, 35 keyframe animations, 15 body skins, 8 eye textures and 14
sounds are from **Animal Garden - Lion 1.0.3**, public domain under The Unlicense. The
jar is a native Fabric 26.2 build carrying Mojang names, so the geometry transferred
verbatim; only its bundled copy of the vanilla animation API needed swapping for
vanilla's own. Its `aquarius_libs` dependency was **not** vendored — every touchpoint
already had an equivalent in Menagerie, so the mod stays dependency-clean.

The eye-layer idea (eyes as a separate emissive pass, left and right independent so a
lion can wink) is theirs and is kept.

## Animal Garden — Western Gorilla (aquarius_playz)

Menagerie's gorilla model, animations, textures and voice come from
**Animal Garden - Western Gorilla 1.0.1** by **aquarius_playz**, released into the
**public domain under The Unlicense**. The jar ships a full `UNLICENSE` file and
declares `license="Unlicense"` in its `META-INF/mods.toml`; both were read and
verified before anything was imported, so copying assets *and* code is cleared
outright. Credit here is courtesy, not obligation — the work deserves it.

What we took, and what we changed:

| Asset | Use in Menagerie |
|-------|------------------|
| `textures/entity/{default,brown,darker}.png` | lowland gorilla fur variants |
| `textures/entity/{black,brown_back}.png` | mountain gorilla fur variants |
| `textures/entity/silverback.png` | translucent silverback saddle overlay (all furs) |
| `sounds/idle1-7.ogg` | `entity.gorilla.ambient` (7 random variants) |
| `sounds/chestpump.ogg` | `entity.gorilla.chest_beat` |
| `ModMobModel` geometry (26 parts, 128×128) | `GorillaModel` |
| `MobAnimations` (28 keyframe definitions) | `GorillaAnimations` |
| `SilverbackLayer` | `SilverbackLayer` (translucent overlay concept) |
| `ModFollowMaleGoal` structure | `FollowSilverbackGoal` (retargeted to our troop ids) |

Porting notes: the source is Forge 1.20.1 with SRG-obfuscated vanilla references.
Every SRG name was resolved against an independent remap table before conversion to
Mojang 26.2 names — the keyframe data itself is untouched. The mod's hand-rolled
animation runner was dropped in favour of 26.2's baked `KeyframeAnimation` API, and
its gender/behaviour-type gating was replaced by Menagerie's own troop, taming and
disposition logic, which remains authoritative.

## Everything else

All other Menagerie art is generated by `tools/gen-textures.js` from palettes sampled
out of vanilla Minecraft textures; all other sounds are pitch-shifted references to
vanilla sound events. See `RESEARCH.md` for the full license review of every mod
studied during development.
